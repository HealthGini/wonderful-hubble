❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is GoodDeeds.space completely free?

A: Yes! GoodDeeds.space is 100% free and open for everyone to promote goodness in their communities.

Q: Do I need an account to view community highlights?

A: No, anyone can browse public Kudos, Posts, and Space activities without logging in.

Q: How do Public Kudos work?

A: Simply select a recipient, write your heartfelt message, and optionally tag a shared space.

Q: How can I create a new Space for my group?

A: Logged-in members can click "+ Create Space" under the Spaces menu to start a community space.
